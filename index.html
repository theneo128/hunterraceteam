Sync Across Devices

Each rider's data lives on their device. Use Export/Import to sync between phones (AirDrop, email, etc.). Merging keeps the higher total time per rider.

Install on iPhone

Add Ride Tracker to your home screen so it opens like a regular app.

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari (square with an arrow).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Done!

Remove rider?

This will erase their ride time. This can't be undone.
