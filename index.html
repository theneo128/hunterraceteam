Install on iOS

iOS doesn't show an install prompt.
Add Race Control to your Home Screen instead:

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari (the square with the arrow).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Race Control will appear like a native app.
